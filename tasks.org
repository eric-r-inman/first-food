* DONE Trim template-workflow overkill for this solo project

Disabled the token-heavy and noise-heavy parts of the rust-template workflow
that do not earn their keep on a private, unreleased, solo game:

- *Per-turn template-compliance review gate* — unwired the Stop hook in
  =.claude/settings.json= (the =review-stop.sh= script and the
  =template-compliance= agent stay in the repo).  This was the main Claude-token
  sink: it spawned a reviewing subagent on every turn that touched code.
- *Dependabot version updates* — removed =.github/dependabot.yml= to stop
  automated dependency PRs.  The now-inert automerge workflow is left in place.
- *Local =cargo-semver-checks=* — dropped from the flake dev shell; it is ABI
  stability tooling for published libraries, and this project is not published.

Kept: the Nix dev shell, =treefmt=, local tests, the structural compliance
checker (=just compliance=, which is cheap and not per-turn), CI, the Elm
frontend, and the server-crate stub.

Each deviation is recorded as a =compliance-ignores= entry in
=rust-template.json=.  Re-enable any one by reverting its change and removing
the matching ignore.
