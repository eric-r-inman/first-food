* Upcoming
** Added
- Browser map editor with a movable viewport, paint/fill/eyedropper tools, drag
  painting, undo/redo, and JSON save/load.
- Data-driven overlay layers edited in their own browser tabs: resources,
  landmarks, buildings, units, weather, and climate.
- Visibility rotation: a tile cycles through every stacked layer one symbol per
  second, with a per-layer toggle to exclude a layer from the cycle.
- Shift-click erases objects in the active layer.
** Fixes
- Box the data-error variant the editor carries so its results stay within the
  size the lint allows.
